# Documentation and ADRs

## ADR Format

- **Title**: `ADR-00X: Short Title`
- **Status**: Proposed / Accepted / Superseded
- **Context**: Why are we making this decision? What problem does it solve?
- **Decision**: What choice was made?
- **Consequences**: What are the trade-offs, advantages, and drawbacks?
